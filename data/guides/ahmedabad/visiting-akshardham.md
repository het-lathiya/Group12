## A place of worship first

Swaminarayan Akshardham in Gandhinagar is a working mandir, and its visitor rules aim to keep a calm, sacred atmosphere. Expect queues at peak seasons and on holidays, and follow the instructions of the security staff.

## Dress code

- Upper clothing must cover the shoulders, chest, navel and upper arms.
- Lower clothing must reach below the knee.
- If your clothes do not meet the code, you can borrow a sarong at the entrance against a refundable deposit. Return it when you leave.

## What you cannot take in

The following are not allowed inside the complex:

- mobile phones, cameras, music players, USB and pen drives and other electronic items;
- luggage;
- food and drink;
- weapons, toys and pets;
- tobacco, alcohol and drugs.

You can carry wallets and purses, shoes, belts, jewellery, umbrellas and jackets. Baby food and water bottles may be checked more closely. Because cameras are not permitted, you cannot take your own photographs inside. Akshardham instead offers a special photo taken at a vantage point on the campus.

## Security and the cloakroom

Everyone passes through metal detectors and may be frisked, and vehicles are searched. A free cloakroom near the entrance takes prohibited items; deposit them before you join the security queue to save time.

- Switch off any electronic items you leave.
- Items must be within a size limit.
- Do not leave money or valuables.
- Everything is left at the owner's risk.

## Help for elderly and disabled visitors

After the security check, wheelchairs are available at the entrance, free of charge, for people with disabilities, elderly visitors and anyone else who needs one. There are restrooms around the campus.

## Food and parking

Outside food is not allowed, but the Premvati food court on the campus serves pure vegetarian meals and snacks. Groups larger than fifty are asked to contact Akshardham in advance. Parking is for visitors only and is charged. Spaces are first come, first served, and goods vehicles are not allowed. Smoking and alcohol are banned on the campus and in the car park, including carrying alcohol in a vehicle.
