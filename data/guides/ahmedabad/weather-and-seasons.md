## Climate in brief

Ahmedabad has a hot semi-arid climate with three main seasons: summer, monsoon and winter. Outside the monsoon the air is very dry. The city is almost flat.

## Summer (March to June)

Summer is long and very hot. Average daytime highs reach about 43 °C, with nights around 24 °C. The highest temperature on record is 48 °C, measured on 20 May 2016. A deadly heat wave in May 2010, when temperatures reached 46.8 °C, led the city to adopt a Heat Action Plan.

## Monsoon (mid-June to mid-September)

The south-west monsoon usually arrives in mid-June and lasts until mid-September, bringing humid weather. July and August are the wettest months. The city receives about 800 mm of rain a year, but it often falls in heavy, sudden downpours that can make local rivers flood. If the monsoon is weak, drought can follow.

## Winter (November to February)

Winter is the coolest season, with average highs of about 30 °C and lows of about 13 °C. In January, cold winds from the north can bring a mild chill. Winter is also the season of Uttarayan, the kite festival held on 14 and 15 January.

## Festivals through the year

Besides Uttarayan in January, the city celebrates Navratri, when Garba is danced for nine nights at venues across the city, as well as Diwali. The Rath Yatra procession from the Jagannath Temple takes place on a date set by the Hindu calendar. Holi, Muharram and Christmas are also celebrated.
