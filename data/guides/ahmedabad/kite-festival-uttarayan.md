## What Uttarayan is

Uttarayan is Gujarat's two-day kite festival, held every January. It is tied to Makar Sankranti, when the sun moves into Capricorn. The festival marks the end of winter and the start of the harvest season. The second day is called Vasi Uttarayan.

## Ahmedabad, the kite capital

Ahmedabad is often called the state's kite capital and hosts the International Kite Festival. In the big cities, kite flying begins very early in the morning and can go on late into the night.

Popular places to watch include:

- **the Sabarmati Riverfront**, whose banks can hold a very large crowd;
- **the Police Stadium**, where spectators lie on the ground to watch the sky fill with kites.

Food stalls and performers set up alongside the kite markets. On the second night, people release illuminated kites called tukkals, which carry small lights or candles into the dark sky.

## Food of the festival

The traditional festival foods are:

- **undhiyu**, a dish of mixed vegetables;
- **chikki**, a brittle of sesame seeds or peanuts set in jaggery;
- **jalebi**, a fried sweet.

## Kite markets

The Patang Bazaar at Raipur Darwaza in Ahmedabad is one of the best-known kite markets. Kite sales rise sharply in the week before the festival, and during the festival the market is busy with bulk buying and bargaining.

## A word of caution

Fighter kites are flown on string coated with glue and ground glass. Once dry, the string has a sharp edge that is meant to cut rival kites, and it can also cut skin. Handle kite string with care.
