## What the walk is

The Ahmedabad Municipal Corporation runs a guided morning Heritage Walk through the old city. It presents around six centuries of the city's history on foot and describes Ahmedabad as India's first World Heritage City. The walk is described as going "from Mandir to Masjid": it starts at a temple and ends at a mosque.

## Route

The walk starts at the Swaminarayan Temple in Kalupur and ends at the Jama Masjid. On the way it passes through several of the old city's pols (traditional residential neighbourhoods) and some of their temples. Stops named on the city's page include:

- Kavi Dalpatram Chowk;
- the Calico Dome;
- the secret passage in Shantinath ni Pol;
- Harkunvar Shethani ni Haveli;
- Astapadji Mandir;
- Fernandes Bridge;
- Manek Chowk;
- Badshah no Hajiro;
- the Jama Masjid.

The city's heritage website also marks which sites have digital guides, such as audio guides, QR codes and virtual tours, in English, Hindi and Gujarati.

## Dress code

The temple at the start and the mosque at the end are active places of worship, so a strict dress code applies inside them:

- clothing must cover the shoulders, chest, navel and upper arms;
- lower garments must reach below the knees.
