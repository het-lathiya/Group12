## Gandhinagar, the twin city

Ahmedabad is Gujarat's commercial centre, but the state capital is Gandhinagar, a twin city about 30 km to the north. An inter-city highway links the two.

## Swaminarayan Akshardham

The Akshardham temple in Gandhinagar is about 35 km from Ahmedabad. Besides the temple itself, it offers shows on Indian heritage during the day and a water show in the evening. Its visitor rules are in the separate Akshardham guide.

## Adalaj stepwell

The Adalaj stepwell, about 18 to 19 km north of Ahmedabad, is a five-storey stepwell built in the late fifteenth and early sixteenth centuries. It blends Hindu and Islamic architectural styles, is known for its fine stone carvings, and has a tragic legend about its origin.

## Nal Sarovar Bird Sanctuary

Nal Sarovar, about 65 km from Ahmedabad, is a large lake with surrounding marshland that attracts many species of birds. The best season to visit is from November to February.

## Thol Bird Sanctuary

Thol, about 30 km west of the city centre, is a lesser-known bird lake. It is popular for early-morning birdwatching trips, and the best months are November to March. There is no public transport to Thol, so hiring a taxi for the trip is recommended.

## Planning tips

Both bird sanctuaries are best visited in the cooler months, from November to February or March. Akshardham, in Gandhinagar, and the Adalaj stepwell both lie north of Ahmedabad.
